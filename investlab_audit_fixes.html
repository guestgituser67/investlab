Understanding Nominal vs. Inflation-Adjusted Returns

Nominal value represents the dollar amount you will see in your bank account in the future. Inflation-adjusted (real) value measures your actual purchasing power in today’s dollars. Because inflation gradually increases the cost of goods over time, a dollar 30 years from now will buy less than a dollar buys today.
